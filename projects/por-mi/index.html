The brief

A phone call should not become a language barrier.

por mí explores a simple experience: describe the call you need, approve its details, and follow the conversation with a transcript and listening controls.

03

A usable result

Project walkthrough · 01

01 / Describe the task

Tell the assistant who to call, which language to use and what needs to be resolved. The brief stays editable before the call.

Project walkthrough · 02

02 / Review before calling

Review the destination and cost estimate. A specific approval is required before a paid call. Editing the task invalidates the old approval.

Project walkthrough · 03

03 / Follow the conversation

Telnyx carries the telephone audio; OpenAI Realtime powers the voice interaction. The interface exposes the transcript and listening controls.

Project walkthrough · 04

04 / Keep the outcome

The prototype keeps call data locally. Production readiness needs further route, language, security and consent testing.
